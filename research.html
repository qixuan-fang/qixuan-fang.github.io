Research

Research interests

My research lies at the intersection of representation theory of vertex algebras and (generalized) complex differential geometry, with a long-term goal towards a mathematical theory of two-dimensional quantum non-linear sigma models.

To develop the QFT on curved target manifolds, the commutativity/locality/skew-symmetry of vertex operators shall be relaxed to adapt curvature. This motivates Huang to define meromorphic open-string algebras (MOSVAs), using which he was able to construct representations of MOSVAs from eigenfunctions of the Laplacian, a successful first step incorporating bosonic quantum mechanics as a part of the theory.
