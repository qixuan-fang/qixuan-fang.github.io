Research

Research interests

My research lies at the intersection of vertex operator algebras, tensor categories, conformal field theory, representation theory, and differential geometry.

I am especially interested in using vertex-operator-algebraic methods to formulate and study rigorous mathematical models of two-dimensional conformal field theories and nonlinear sigma models.

Current projects

Vertex operator algebras and sigma models

I am working on constructions relating vertex operator algebras, modules, and geometric structures arising from nonlinear sigma models.

Tensor categories and conformal field theory

I am interested in tensor-categorical structures in representation theory of vertex operator algebras and their role in conformal field theory.
