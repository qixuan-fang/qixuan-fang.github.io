Research

My research lies in mathematical physics, with a focus on vertex operator algebras, tensor categories, conformal field theory, and their interactions with geometry.

Research interests

Vertex operator algebras

Vertex-algebraic structures, modules, intertwining operators, and tensor-categorical constructions.

Conformal field theory

Mathematical constructions of two-dimensional conformal field theories and related sigma models.

Tensor categories

Tensor product theory for modules, categorical structures, and representation-theoretic methods.

Geometry

Geometric input from differential geometry and complex geometry in field-theoretic constructions.

Selected current directions

VOA approach to nonlinear sigma models

Developing vertex-operator-algebraic structures that encode geometric and analytic data appearing in two-dimensional nonlinear sigma models.

Tensor products and intertwining operators

Understanding how categorical tensor products and intertwining operators organize the representation theory of vertex operator algebras.
