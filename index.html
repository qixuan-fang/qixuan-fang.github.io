About me

Welcome to my homepage. My name is Qixuan Fang (方啓軒/方启轩). I am currently a Ph.D. candidate in mathematics at Rutgers, The State University of New Jersey–Newark. My advisor is Yi-Zhi Huang.

I do research in mathematical physics. I am interested in the mathematical construction of quantum field theories, especially conformal field theories, via vertex algebras, representation theory, and tensor categories, and in their interactions with geometry. In particular, I am currently working on a vertex-operator-algebraic approach toward constructing two-dimensional nonlinear sigma models.

Brief academic history

  • 09/2019–06/2023: B.Sc. in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics, Nanjing University.
  • 09/2023–present: Ph.D. candidate, Rutgers University–Newark.
