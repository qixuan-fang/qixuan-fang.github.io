About me

Welcome to my homepage. My name is Qixuan Fang (方啓軒 / 方启轩). I am currently a Ph.D. candidate in mathematics at Rutgers, The State University of New Jersey–Newark. My advisor is Yi-Zhi Huang.

I do research in mathematical physics. I am interested in mathematical construction of quantum field theories, especially in two-dimension, via vertex algebras and their variants, representation theory, tensor categories, and their interactions with geometry.

Brief academic history

  • 09/2019–06/2023: B.Sc. in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics, Nanjing University.
  • 09/2023–present: Ph.D. candidate, Rutgers University–Newark.
