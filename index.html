Welcome to my homepage. My name is Qixuan Fang (方啓軒/方启轩). I am currently a Ph.D. candidate in mathematics at Rutgers, The State University of New Jersey–Newark.

I do research in mathematical physics, especially the mathematical construction of quantum field theories and conformal field theories through vertex algebras, representation theory, tensor categories, and geometry.

About me

My advisor is Yi-Zhi Huang. In particular, I am currently working on a vertex-operator-algebraic approach toward constructing two-dimensional nonlinear sigma models.

Research areas

Vertex operator algebras, tensor categories, conformal field theory, mathematical physics, and geometric structures related to nonlinear sigma models.

Teaching

I have taught calculus, linear algebra, intermediate algebra, and recitation sections at Rutgers–Newark.

Brief academic history

  • 09/2019–06/2023. B.Sc. in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics, Nanjing University.
  • 09/2023–present. Ph.D. candidate in Mathematics, Rutgers University–Newark.
