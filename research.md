<h1>Research</h1>

<section class="page-section">
  <h2>Overview</h2>
  <p>
    My research is in mathematical physics, with emphasis on vertex operator algebras, conformal field theory, tensor categories, representation theory, and their interaction with geometry.
  </p>
  <p>
    A current direction of my work is to study vertex-operator-algebraic approaches to two-dimensional nonlinear sigma models. More broadly, I am interested in how algebraic structures from conformal field theory can encode geometric and analytic data.
  </p>
</section>

<section class="page-section">
  <h2>Current interests</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>Vertex operator algebras and their modules.</li>
    <li>Intertwining operators and tensor category structures.</li>
    <li>Geometric constructions of conformal field theories.</li>
    <li>Nonlinear sigma models and their algebraic structures.</li>
    <li>Connections between representation theory, geometry, and quantum field theory.</li>
  </ul>
</section>
