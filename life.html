Non-academic life

Sports

Sports are an indispensable part of my life. Here is a brief sports history of mine:

  • I have played football ("soccer" in American English) since 2010, after watching the FIFA World Cup that year. Since then, I have represented the following schools as a member of their school teams, in chronological order:
    • No. 1 Affiliated Primary School of Lianyungang Normal College, where I learned some basics of football. My coach at the time was Liming Sun.
    • Lianyungang Foreign Language School, where I played as a left-wing striker in my first year. However, because I grew very quickly, my coach at the time, Kejie Sun, moved me to center back. I have been a center back since then.
    • Jiangsu Xinhai Senior High School, where we played in the city's Mayor's Cup without a coach due to administrative disorder.
    • Nanjing University.
  • I have played basketball regularly since 2016. After watching the Cleveland Cavaliers in 2015 and 2016, the player I have admired and imitated the most ever since is Kyrie Irving.
  • I trained quite intensively in table tennis, or ping-pong, in 2011 and 2012. I play with a Chinese penhold grip. Many techniques that were mainstream at the time, such as the penhold backhand block, have since fallen out of favor. But I still play intensively once in a while.
  • I learned to swim in the summer of 2012. Since then, I have continued swimming on and off. I mainly swim freestyle and breaststroke. I can swim continuously, but I do not have the technique to swim fast. Overall, I would describe myself as only a beginner in swimming. By the way, the swimming facilities of Rutgers University on both the Newark campus and the New Brunswick campus are fabulous.
  • I was coached in badminton on and off by my father starting around 2012, as he played semi-professionally during the 2010s. I still enjoy playing badminton.
  • My go-to cardio exercise is indoor cycling, as well as outdoor cycling when the weather is sunny.
  • I have played a lot of pool, thanks to the very approachable tables that have kept appearing in different living environments of mine. However, I am a very bad player!
  • Recently, I started playing pickleball. It is so fun, almost as fun as watching tennis!
  • ......

On a more serious note, I have always believed that sportsmanship has shaped who I am. Optimism, fairness, competition, friendship, and health are among the most important things in life to me. With that being said, if you are interested in playing any of the above sports with me, please do not hesitate to contact me.
