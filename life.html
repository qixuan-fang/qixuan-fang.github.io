Non-academic life

Sports

Sports are an indispensable part of my life. Here is a brief sports history of mine:

  • I have played football ("soccer" in American English since 2010, after watching the FIFA World Cup that year. Since then, I have represented the following schools as a member of their school teams, in chronological order:
    • No. 1 Affiliated Primary School of Lianyungang Normal College, where I learned a bit basics about football. My coach then was Liming Sun.
    • Lianyungang Foreign Language School, where I played as a left-wing striker in my first year, but my height grew too fast, my coach then Kejie Sun moved me to a center back. I have been a center back since then.
    • Jiangsu Xinhai Senior High School, where we played Major's Cup of the City with no coach due to administrative disorders.
    • Nanjing University.
  • I started to play basketball regularly since 2016. Because of Cleveland Cavaliers' games I watched in 2015 and 2016, the player I adore and imitate the most ever since is Kyrie Irving.
  • I was trained playing table tennis (ping-pong) quite intensively during 2011 and 2012. I play Chinese penhold. Many techniques that were mainstream at the time, such as the penhold backhand block, have since fallen out of favor. But I still play intensively once in a while.
  • I learned to swim in summer 2012. Since then, I have continued swimming on and off. I mainly swim freestyle and breaststroke. I can swim continuously but do not have the techniques to be fast. Overall I claim only to be a beginner in swimming. By the way, the swimming facilities of Rutgers University on both Newark campus and New Brunswick campus are fabulous.
  • I was coached playing badminton by my father on and off since maybe 2012, as he played semi-professionally in the 2010's decade. I still enjoy playing badminton.
  • My go-to aerobics is indoor cycling, and outdoor cycling, provided it being a sunny day.
  • I played a lot of Pool games, thanks to very approachable tables that keep appearing in different living environments of mine. However, I am a very bad player!
  • Recently, I started to play Pickleball. It is so fun, almost as fun as (watching) tennis!
  • ......
On a more serious note, I have always believed that sportsmanship has shaped who I am. Optimism, fairness, competition, friendship, and health are among the most important things in life to me. With that being said, if you are interested in playing any of the above with me, please do not hesitate to contact me.

Chinese calligraphy

Chinese calligraphy, especially pen calligraphy, is another major passion of mine. My favorite calligrapher, and the one whose works I have copied most often, is Wang Xizhi, arguably the greatest calligrapher of all time.
