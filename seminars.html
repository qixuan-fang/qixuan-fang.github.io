Rutgers–Newark Graduate Mathematics Seminar

I am currently the organizer of the Rutgers–Newark Graduate Mathematics Seminar (RNGMS), kindly supported by Graduate Student Government Association (GSGA).

All are welcome, whether or not you are from Rutgers. Talks range from expository introductions to research-level topics in mathematics, and more. If you are interested in giving a talk, please contact me, or write to graduatemath543@gmail.com.

Spring 2026 special session: mini-course on homological algebra .
